## Cantonese Gospel Ministry on the North Shore

- North Shore Cantonese service: Sundays 3:30 PM
- North Shore prayer meeting: Sundays 2:45 PM
- Peter Fellowship (Cantonese): 2nd and 4th Sunday, 4:30 PM

## Why the North Shore

Holy Word Church was founded in 1994 to bring the gospel to different communities. The North Shore ministry continues that calling by reaching Cantonese-speaking migrants who live north of the harbour, starting with care for the local community.

## What We Hold To

We are an evangelical church. We hold to the fundamental truths of the Christian faith and to the church's [Statement of Faith](page:beliefs).
