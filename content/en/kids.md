## Children's Ministry

Children are grouped by age and led by trained teachers. They get to know God through songs, Bible stories and small group activities. Parents can drop children off at the classroom ten minutes before the service and pick them up from the same room afterwards.

- **Ages:** 3 years to Year 6
- **Where:** Classrooms in the extension building
- **First visit:** Please sign your child in at the welcome desk and a teacher will show you to the classroom

## Youth Ministry

- **Ages:** Year 7 to Year 13
- **Meets:** Sundays alongside the English service, plus a Friday night event once a month
- **Language:** Mainly English

## Child Safety

All teachers are safety-checked and trained under the church's child protection policy.
