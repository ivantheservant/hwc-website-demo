## Where and How to Join

- **Where:** Unless stated otherwise, fellowships meet at the church (2 Basalt Place, East Tamaki). The North Shore group meets at the North Shore service venue.
- **Contact:** Call or email the church office and we will pass your details to the group leader.
- **First visit:** No need to register. Just come along at the meeting time and someone will welcome you.
