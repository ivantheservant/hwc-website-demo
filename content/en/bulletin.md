**Giving**

Let us remember the joy of giving and the blessings it brings to both the giver and the receiver. Tithe offering is a duty of all Christians. By giving, we honour God and fulfill His work through our church. May God bless the offering we willingly give. For more details on how you can give, please visit our church website: [https://holywordchurch.org.nz/en/giving](page:give).

---

**Prayer Items for this Week**

- Give thanks that Jesus, the beloved Son, gave His body for us — "given for you." Pray that we would receive this gift with gratitude and remember Him often.
- Pray that, as people who are taken, blessed and broken by God's hand, we would not hold on to our lives for ourselves, but let them be given for others.
- Pray for courage to take up our cross daily and follow Jesus — in our homes, our workplaces and our community — so that others may taste His love through us.
