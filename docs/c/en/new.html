First Time Here

Demo content This page is demo content. The text only illustrates the layout and is not official church information.

Welcome! Visiting a church for the first time can raise a few questions. This page gives you an idea of what to expect before you come.

Which service is for me?

We have four services on Sunday. They are similar in content and differ in language. Choose the one you are most comfortable with. There is no need to register.

English9:00 AM2 Basalt Place, East TamakiCantonese10:45 AM2 Basalt Place, East TamakiMandarin2:30 PM2 Basalt Place, East TamakiCantonese (North Shore)3:30 PMSee North Shore page for venue

What is a service like?

  • A service runs for about 75 to 90 minutes, with singing, prayer, Bible reading and a sermon.
  • Come in tidy, comfortable clothes. There is no need to dress formally.
  • Giving is for church members. Visitors should not feel any pressure to give.
  • Our ushers will welcome you at the door and help you find a seat.

Location and Parking

We are at 2 Basalt Place, East Tamaki, Auckland. The church has its own car park. When it is full, you can park along Basalt Place.

View on map

Bringing Children

We have children's worship and children's Sunday school on Sundays. See Children & Youth.

Common Questions

I am not a Christian. Can I come?

Yes. Our services are open to everyone. You are welcome to sit and listen, and no one will ask you to do anything.

I do not understand Cantonese. What should I do?

We have Cantonese, Mandarin and English services. Choose the one that suits you. See Service Times.

I live on the North Shore. Is there a service closer to me?

Yes. There is a Cantonese service on the North Shore on Sundays at 3:30 PM. See North Shore.

How do I get baptised or join the church?

Please speak to the pastor of your congregation, or leave a message through the Contact page and one of our staff will talk with you.

We Would Love to Meet You

Stay for a cup of tea after the service. You can also fill in the visitor form and one of our staff will get in touch.
