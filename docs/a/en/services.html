english

Join us as we worship in the spirit and in truth every Sunday starting at 9 am, then followed by prayer meeting at 10:30 am. For those that want to learn more regarding the bible knowledge, come along to our TNT classes at 11 am.

Contact us for small group meeting times.

CHILDREN

A great place for kids to worship, learn about the Word and bond with each other. Starts at 10:45 am in the extension building.

CANTONESE/ MANDARIN

Our other congregations have Cantonese and Mandarin services starting at 10:45 am and 2:30 pm respectively.
